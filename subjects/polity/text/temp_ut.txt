5. Union and its territories
            * Art 1- india , ie bharat = union of states, not federation. 
            * Concept taken from = British North American Act (canadian constitution)
            * Territory types- UTs; States; Acquired territories
            * States= no right to secede - India is a nation "holding together"
            * Art 2 parliament = power admit + estb new states that were not previously part of India.
            * No state role
            * Art 3 - Change in state or UTs, internal re-adjustment ( ex-creation of telangana)
            * Increase/ decrease area 
            * Uniting 2 or more states
            * 18th CAA = amended Art 3 - incl UT in definition of 'state' + empower Parliament to form new state or UT by uniting part of any state or UT. Ex: Ladakh was formed with a simple majority in Parliament under Article 3, after the J&K Reorganisation Act, 2019.

SK Dhar commission and JVP committee
            * SK dhar = 1948- constituent assembly appointed. The Commission suggested administrative ease rather Linguistic factor.
            * JVP committee (dec 1948) - Jawaharlal Nehru, Vallabhbhai Patel, and Pattabhi Sitaramayya. Formally rejected language as a basis for  re- organization.
            * Linguistic identity got momentum as seen in movements for Aikya Kerala, Samyukta MH & Vishalandhra
            * Forced to create first linguistic state, 1953 Andhra, separating, Telugu speaking area from Madras, followed by date of popular vegetation and death of Potti Sriramulu  (visalandhra movement)
 
Fazl Ali Commission or state reorg commission ( SRC ) 1953
            * Creation of Andhra intensified demand from other regions, forcing to from 3 member state reorganisation Commission-  chairman Justice Fazl Ali, pannikar & Hridaynath Kunzru
            * Accepted language as basis of reorganization. 
            * But Rejected theory of one language, one state. 
            * Unity of India= primary consideration in retaining the country's political unity.
            * 4 factors in any scheme of reorganization.
            * Preservation, strengthening- unity, security of country (Security)
            * linguistic and cultural homogeneity (Culture and Language)
            * Financial, economic and administrative consideration. (Economy)
            * Planning, promotion of people welfare in state. (Social)
            * Suggested abolition of 4 fold classifications under original constitution and creation of 16 states, 3 administered territory.
            * Gov't accepted with minor modifications. 14 states created November 1, 1956. (7th caa)
