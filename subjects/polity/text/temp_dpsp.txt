Case
	Judgment
	Impact
	Champakam Dorairajan (1951)
	DPSPs are subsidiary to FRs but FRs can be amended.
	Led to 1st  (1951), 4th (1955), & 17th (1964) Amendments to implement some DPSPs.
(art 31A and 9th schedule)
	Golak Nath (1967)
	FRs are sacrosanct.
Parliament cannot amend FRs to imple DPSPs. 
	Parliament reacted with: 
24th CAA (1971) → Gave power to amend FRs. 25th CAA (1971) inserted art 31 © → Laws implementing Article 39(b) & (c) DPSPs cannot be challenged for violating FRs (Art 14, 19). Courts cannot question such laws.
	Kesavananda bharati case 1973
	Declaring 2nd provision of art 31 C unconstitutional on grounds Judicial review is a basic feature of the constitution.
	Parliament reacted with 42nd CAA accorded primacy to DPSPs.
	Minerva Mills (1980)
	Struck down restrictions on FRs. Indian consti is founded on the bedrock of balance between FR + DPSP = Constitution’s conscience.
	Declared that FRs are not absolute but also not fully subordinate to DPSPs.
	IR Coelho (2007)
	Balanced FR & DPSPs. Judicial review of 9th Schedule allowed.
	Nothing in the 9th Schedule can abrogate FRs. Currently, FRs hold primacy over DPSPs but parlia can amend FRs to give effect to DPSPs w/o damaging basic structure doctrine.
