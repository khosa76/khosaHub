# DPSP's (Directive Principles of State Policy)

**Part-IV (Article 36-51)**

---

## Basics

1. Non Justiciable in Nature.


2. They were Made on recommendation of Tej Bahadur Sapru Committee.


3. They are mostly Borrowed from Ireland (Irish constitution).


4. Unlike Fundamental Rights which are mostly negative (-ve) in Connotation, DPSP's have a positive (+ve) concept of welfare State.


5. It promotes inclusive growth.


6. According to Dr. Ambedkar, these are the "Novel Features" of the Constitution.


7. According to Granville Austin, DPSP's are the "Conscience of the Constitution".


8. DPSP's are enabling in Nature; they enable government for comprehensive programs.


9. These Focus on Social & Economic Justice, unlike Fundamental Rights which Focus on Political Justice.


10. Ressemble the "Instruments of Instruction" Under government of India Act 1935.

**Classification of DPSP'S (By Dr. M. P. Sharma)**

### 1. Gandhian

* **Characteristics:** Focus on grassroot Democracy | Non-Conventional

* Organisation of PRI (Panchayati Raj Institutions)


* Promotion of Co-operative Societies


* Promotion of education & economic interest of SC, ST & weaker Sections


* Organisation of agriculture & animal husbandry


* Prohibition of consumption of intoxicating drinks & Drugs





### 2. Socialist

* **Characteristics:** Conventional in Economic terms | Egalitarian (Opportunity) | *Mostly economic things


* **Key Provisions:**
* State to Secure Social order for promotion of welfare of people


* Article 39


* Right to work, education & Public assistance in certain cases


* Humane Conditions of work & Maternity relief


* Participation of workers in Management of Industries


* Duty of State to raise the Level of Nutrition & Standard of Living





### 3. Liberal-Intellectual

* **Characteristics:** Self-Development

## Traps of DPSP's

1. **DPSP's are optional for state.** **[❌ Incorrect]**

* *(Correction: Although they are Non-enforceable in Court, they are an obligation to the State)*



2. **DPSP's Belong ONLY to socialist ideology.** **[❌ Incorrect]**

* *(Correction: They are also based on Gandhian & Liberal-Intellectual ideologies)*



3. **Scope of DPSP's have never changed.** **[❌ Incorrect]**

* *(Correction: It has been expanded via Amendments like 42nd, 44th, 86th & 97th)*



4. **DPSP'S were Borrowed ONLY from Ireland.** **[❌ Incorrect]**

* *(Correction: They are borrowed majorly from Ireland, but are also influenced by Spain & US constitution)*



5. **DPSP's override Fundamental Rights.** **[❌ Incorrect]**

* *(Correction: They prevail in harmony)*



6. **DPSP's Bind ONLY Parliament.** **[❌ Incorrect]**

* *(Correction: It binds both Center & State & whatever comes under state)*



7. **DPSP'S & Fundamental Rights have NO relation.** **[❌ Incorrect]**

* *(Correction: Together they form the Justice under the Preamble)*



8. **DPSP's cannot Lead to enforceable Rights.** **[❌ Incorrect]**

* *(Correction: Free Legal Aid, Education Right, PRI – Many Fundamental Rights have evolved from DPSP's)*



9. **Courts cannot use DPSP'S.** **[❌ Incorrect]**

* *(Correction: Courts can use DPSP's for interpretation)*



10. **DPSP'S are Legally useless.** **[❌ Incorrect]**

* *(Correction: Supreme Court calls them fundamental in governance of India)*
